# SOLA v7.10.0: Release Notes, Feature Summary, and 20-minute Admin Walkthrough

## Part 1: Release Notes (18 October 2026)

## v7.10.0 (18 October 2026): a settings menu organized by task

**Why this release exists.** An audit of the settings found 289 changeable settings on 11 flat pages, and 178 of them (62%) had never been changed on Learn, Degrees, dev or alpha. Finding the one you wanted meant scrolling past all the others. This release regroups the menu by what you're trying to do. It's a move, not a cleanup: no setting was renamed, deleted or given a new default.

**Five task pages and an Advanced group.** Under Site administration > Plugins > Local plugins > AI Course Assistant, the 11 flat pages are replaced by five task pages and an **Advanced** group of five more. The task pages carry 129 settings and the Advanced pages carry the other 160.

| Page | Settings | What's on it |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Essentials** | 22 | Switch SOLA on, default course mode and auto-open, branding and avatar, contact and privacy details, FAQ and conversation starters, footer links. The version banner and the quick links to the tool pages sit here too |
| **Models and keys** | 24 | The Providers and keys table, the default provider, model, token limit, reasoning effort and system prompt, model roles (quiz, essay, mastery classifier), the premium tier, failover and retries |
| **Features** | 43 | Pedagogy defaults, mastery and learning paths, study plans and reminders, the voice tab, surveys, practice scoring, usability testing, active learners and outreach |
| **Safety and privacy** | 16 | Quiz lock, hiding on quizzes, off-topic limits, wellbeing, integrity checks and data retention |
| **Cost and reliability** | 24 | Spend caps and their notify emails, cost and answer-rate alerts, the anomaly digest, automatic model upgrades |
| **Advanced > Retrieval** | 21 | Retrieval on or off, external resources, embeddings, search tuning |
| **Advanced > Reranking and documents** | 24 | Reranking, switching embedding models, which document types are read into the index, content size limits |
| **Advanced > Prompt, voice and display** | 42 | Model call details (API Base URL, temperature), prompt budget and debugging, history and learner profile, Voice Mode, voice and speech providers, self-hosted speech-to-text, widget appearance and position |
| **Advanced > Integrations and governance** | 41 | Support and Zendesk, analytics export, CDN, remote configuration, vendor data, price drift, signed policy bundles, benchmarks, recommendations, optimizer weights |
| **Advanced > Soapbox, avatars and legacy** | 32 | Deprecated Soapbox, talking-avatar vendors, and the deprecated `prompt_budget_auto_tune` |

Every page opens with a row of links to the other pages, with the page you're on marked and one sentence saying what it's for. An Advanced page has a second row for the five Advanced pages. The Site administration search box finds a setting on any of the ten pages by its name or label.

**Nothing was renamed, deleted or re-defaulted.** A test records the name and default of all 289 settings as they were before the move and checks them after. Only the page changed, so a site that configures nothing new behaves the same. What stopped showing is the old in-page table of contents, the section anchors it linked to, and the "AI Provider & Conversation" heading above the provider table. No setting is among them. The quick links (analytics, audit log, model registry, Emergency Controls and the rest) are still at the top of Essentials.

**Where a moved setting went.**

| Looking for | Now on |
|-------------|--------|
| Enable plugin, default course mode, auto-open, branding names, avatar, welcome message, contact and privacy details, FAQ, footer links | Essentials |
| Providers and keys table, default provider, model, system prompt, model roles, premium tier, failover and retries | Models and keys |
| Feature switches (mastery, flashcards, study plans, surveys, usability testing, outreach) | Features |
| Quiz lock, off-topic limits, wellbeing, integrity checks, retention | Safety and privacy |
| Spend caps, cost and answer-rate alerts, anomaly digest, automatic model upgrades | Cost and reliability |
| Retrieval on or off, embeddings, search tuning | Advanced > Retrieval |
| Reranking, embedding migration, document types read into the index | Advanced > Reranking and documents |
| API Base URL, temperature, prompt budget, history, Voice Mode, speech providers, widget position | Advanced > Prompt, voice and display |
| Support and Zendesk, Redash export, CDN, remote configuration, policy bundle, vendor data, price drift, benchmarks | Advanced > Integrations and governance |
| Soapbox and talking avatars | Advanced > Soapbox, avatars and legacy |

The policy bundle, price drift and the Support and Zendesk settings are on Advanced > Integrations and governance, not on Cost and reliability or Features. Pages are balanced by the measured size of their show/hide data. With those settings, Cost and Features would have carried 1,185 and 1,001 characters against a ceiling of 950 (core's own limit is 1,024), and in testing the all-settings hub page returned HTTP 500 at developer debug. Integrations and governance carries almost no show/hide rules, so every rule still fires. Soapbox and the talking-avatar settings are on Advanced > Soapbox, avatars and legacy. That's placement, not hiding: a site where only one course turns Soapbox on still finds its settings in one place, and no talking-avatar setting was removed.

**Old settings URLs redirect.** Ten hidden pages are registered under the old page names. A bookmark such as `admin/settings.php?section=local_ai_course_assistant_ai` sends you to the page named for the same topic: general and branding to Essentials, ai to Models and keys, content to Retrieval, rerank and extraction to Reranking and documents, operations to Cost and reliability, engagement and feedback to Features, and integrations to Integrations and governance. The old `safety` page id is still a real page (now Safety and privacy), so it needs no redirect. A redirect lands on one page, but the settings of an old page can now be on several, because the old pages were cut at arbitrary points. If something isn't where you expect, use the search box or the table above. A settings form left open across the upgrade and then saved to an old page id is redirected without saving anything. The plugin's own links point at the new pages. The RAG index page's **Content sources** link now goes to Advanced > Reranking and documents.

**More options on the course page.** On each course's AI settings page, the monthly spend cap, API base URL, temperature and system prompt are now under a **More options** section. It stays closed unless one of the four already has a value. Provider, API key and model stay where they were, and the form sends the same fields as before.

**A settings usage report.** `admin/cli/settings_usage_report.php` prints every SOLA setting on the site it runs on as `default`, `changed`, `secret set`, `secret unset` or `not stored`. It compares the stored value with the registered default by hash, so it never prints a value, and for a secret it only says whether one is set. The text report gives a summary, then one line per changed setting and per secret that is set. `--per-course` adds counts of per-course override settings and per-course config rows. `--tables` adds exact row counts for the plugin's tables. `--json` prints the whole report as JSON. It exists so that a later release can remove settings on evidence. Nothing is removed automatically.

**Also fixed.**

- The model upgrades page, its emails and its refusal messages had no label for the essay-scoring role (`autoupgrade:role_essay`), so they raised a missing-string error at developer debug. The role now reads "Essay scoring". A test checks that every role has a label.

**What the upgrade does to your site.** Nothing. There's no upgrade step, no schema change and no data change. The version bump only makes Moodle rebuild the admin tree. **After you upgrade, purge caches.** The new template markup, strings and CSS are cache-backed, so an old cache can show stale markup and strings.

**Decisions behind this release.**

- A regrouped menu plus an Advanced group, not a global Simple/Advanced switch. A switch would add state to every page, and one can still be added on top of this layout.
- Advanced is five pages, not one, because core limits a page's show/hide data to 1,024 characters. One page would have lost rules.
- Soapbox and talking avatars are placed on Advanced, not hidden behind a toggle.
- The spend caps and their notify emails stay together on Cost and reliability.
- Saved secrets for features that are switched off are left alone. This release clears nothing.
- Settings that were never changed aren't deleted. The usage report comes first, so the evidence can be collected.
- The new admin strings are English only for now (see Known limits).
- Marketplace upload isn't part of this release.

**Known limits.**

- The 39 new admin strings (page titles and intros, the navigation label, section headings, "Moved" and "More options") and the "Essay scoring" role label show in English in every language. They're listed in `KNOWN_UNTRANSLATED`, the same approach as 7.8.9 and 7.9.1. A translation batch follows. The Safety page's English title changed from "Safety and moderation" to "Safety and privacy". The other 45 languages still show their translation of the old title until then.
- The all-settings hub page (`admin/category.php?category=local_ai_course_assistant`) shows the five task pages in one form (129 settings). **Advanced** appears there as a link to its own combined page (160 settings). Nothing is shown twice, and both load at developer debug. As before, change settings from the individual pages. This release doesn't claim to fix the 7.8.1 provider flip, whose cause was never reproduced.
- The old page titles and `sec_*` strings are unused now but are still in the language files.

**Production.** Saylor's production sites (Learn and Degrees) stay on 7.5.7, frozen until 2027. 7.10.0 reaches them only when Catalyst upgrades them. This release goes to the dev fleet (dev.sylr.org and alpha.sylr.org) and GitHub. The Marketplace upload isn't part of it.

### Testing

- `settings_layout_test` checks that every setting has exactly one home, that no show/hide rule is skipped, that each page stays within the 950-character ceiling, that the five task pages carry at most 135 settings (the layout has 129) and none more than 50, that no secret field sits on Essentials, that none of the 289 original defaults changed and none of those settings went missing (a setting added later is allowed), and that all ten redirect pages exist, are hidden and point at real pages.
- Every test that walks the settings pages now also walks the Advanced group, so the no-secret-in-page render covers all ten pages.
- The usage report has 47 tests (a default setting, a changed one, a set secret, a missing row, a multicheckbox default, the per-course counts and the table counts). Two of them guard the `--json` output: one scans the CLI script so it can only print the report, and one plants a secret that can't be decrypted and checks that no developer notice escapes. The rules that matter most, such as never printing a value, were each proven by planting a leak and watching a test fail.
- No chat, prompt, provider or retrieval code changed in this release.
- Release step, not filled in yet: the full suite on the release commit, the CI result, and the dev and alpha deploy check (build number, a render of all ten pages for key values, an old-URL redirect).

Plugin version `2026101801`, release `7.10.0`.

## Part 2: Key SOLA Features

What the plugin does as of v7.10.0, for an admin deciding what to switch on.

- **A tutor that knows the course.** Answers come from the course itself, with
  optional semantic retrieval over pages, books, PDF, DOCX, PPTX, H5P and SCORM
  content and video transcripts.
- **Practice built in.** Practice quizzes, flashcards, worked examples, study
  plans with reminders, and essay feedback against a rubric. Practice only,
  never a Moodle grade.
- **Steps back during assessment.** While a learner has a quiz attempt open, the
  assistant is unavailable to them everywhere, including outside the course.
- **Support outside courses** (optional). On the dashboard and site pages it
  answers enrolment, certificate and technical questions from a support course
  and the FAQ, with its own three starters, and can hand off to Zendesk.
- **Readable answers.** Headings, lists, tables, quotes and code render in the
  chat, and an answer that runs out of room says so rather than stopping
  silently.
- **46 languages**, with automatic reply-in-your-language, speech input and
  output, and an optional live voice mode. Works on phones, including with
  Moodle's menu drawer open.
- **Any provider, one table of keys.** Gemini, OpenAI, Claude (including Opus
  5.5 and Sonnet 5.5), DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, MiniMax, OpenRouter, Together,
  Ollama, Moodle's own AI subsystem, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Every
  provider's key, models and base URL live in one **Providers and keys** table,
  so you can add or change a key without touching the default provider.
  Embedding, Rerank and Voice can use a row's key instead of a pasted copy.
  Failover, a premium tier for hard turns (Claude Sonnet 5, benchmark-backed),
  and spend caps per course.
- **Keeps up with new models.** It knows what each model accepts from data
  rather than name checks, fixes a rejected request by itself, and (unless an
  admin turns it off) tests newer models nightly and switches to one that's
  the same price or cheaper and at least as good, rolling back if live answers
  get worse.
- **Costs you can see.** A model registry with prices and their sources, a daily
  price-drift check, token analytics, cost anomaly alerts and benchmarks.
- **Safe defaults.** Nothing contacts a third party until an admin configures
  it: remote configuration, the Python sandbox runtime and the CDN delivery all
  default to off. The assistant opens by itself only on course pages, and only
  if an admin turns auto-open on.
- **Keys encrypted at rest, and never shown.** Provider keys, the Voice
  providers table, webhook URLs and every other secret are stored with Moodle's
  own encryption, so a database backup alone doesn't reveal them. No settings
  page ever shows a key: a saved one reads "Saved, ends in ab12", and the
  settings-changed notice and Moodle's configuration log hide secrets too.
- **White-label.** Four settings rename the whole product, in every language.
- **Instructor views.** Analytics, a review queue for learner feedback, Learning
  Radar, transcripts with pseudonyms, and surveys with validated rating scales.
- **Python sandbox** (per course). Runs in the learner's browser, never on the
  server, and shows the full error when code fails.
- **Soapbox** remains deprecated, and no release removes it until PresenterAI can
  import existing recordings and scores; its replacement is the standalone
  PresenterAI activity.

## Part 3: 20-minute Admin Walkthrough Script (conversational)

This is written to be read aloud while you click. It assumes a test site on 7.10.0 (the dev fleet is the place for it), an admin login, one provider key you can paste, and shell access to the server for step 8. For the key-table steps in full (a failed save, switching the default provider, the config-log audit), see the 7.9.0 walkthrough.

### 1. Clear the caches and open the new menu (about 2 minutes)

"First thing after any upgrade: purge caches, because the new menu uses cached template markup, strings and CSS." Do that under **Site administration, Development, Purge caches**. Then open **Site administration, Plugins, Local plugins, AI Course Assistant**.

"There used to be eleven pages here. Now there are five: **Essentials**, **Models and keys**, **Features**, **Safety and privacy** and **Cost and reliability**. Under them is an **Advanced** group with five more, and then the tool pages, as before." Nothing was renamed, so a setting you knew by its label still has that label.

### 2. Tour the five task pages (about 3 minutes)

Open **Essentials**. "Every page opens with the same row: the five task pages and Advanced, the page you're on marked, and one sentence saying what it's for." Under it is the version banner with the quick links to the tool pages, Emergency Controls among them. Then the sections: **General** (switch SOLA on, default course mode, auto-open), **Branding**, **Contact and privacy**, **FAQ and conversation starters**, and **Footer Links**. "No key, token or webhook is on this page. A test makes sure of that."

Click **Models and keys**. The **Providers and keys** table is at the top, then **Default provider** (the provider, model, token limit, reasoning effort and system prompt), **Model roles** (quiz, essay, mastery classifier), the premium tier, and **Failover and retries**. Click **Features** and toggle **Enable surveys**, without saving: "A feature's own options appear when you turn it on." Click **Safety and privacy** for quiz lock, off-topic limits, wellbeing, integrity and retention. Click **Cost and reliability**: "All the spend caps are together here, with the notify emails, the alerts, and automatic model upgrades." Put the survey box back as you found it, and don't save.

### 3. Find a setting that moved (about 2 minutes)

"Say you remember a setting by name and not by page." Type `rag_topk` in the Site administration search box. The result is on **Retrieval**, under Advanced. Search matches the setting's name, label and description on all ten pages.

Now open `admin/settings.php?section=local_ai_course_assistant_ai` in the address bar, the old **AI provider and models** page. It sends you to **Models and keys**. "Old bookmarks still work. They land on the page named for the same topic. But the old pages were cut at odd points, so a setting from one of them can be on a different page. Search is the sure way to find it." The other nine old page names redirect the same way.

### 4. Open the Advanced group (about 2 minutes)

Click **Advanced** in the row. It opens **Retrieval**, and now there's a second row with the five Advanced pages. "Most sites never change these." Click through them. **Retrieval** has the on switch, embeddings and search tuning. **Reranking and documents** has reranking, embedding migration and which document types are read. **Prompt, voice and display** has model call details (API Base URL and temperature are here now), history, Voice Mode, speech providers and the widget's look. **Integrations and governance** has Support and Zendesk, the analytics export, CDN, remote configuration, signed policy bundles, vendor data, price drift and benchmarks. **Soapbox, avatars and legacy** has the deprecated Soapbox settings and the talking-avatar vendors.

"Soapbox and avatars are placed here, not hidden. If one course uses Soapbox, you still find its settings in one place."

### 5. Add a key without touching the default (about 3 minutes)

Back on **Models and keys**. "Say you want a Claude key on file for failover, but you don't want to disturb chat." In the **Providers and keys** table press **+ Add provider**. Pick **Claude (Anthropic)**, paste the key into the empty box, and type a model name from the provider's list in **Models**. Leave **Default chat AI provider**, under the **Default provider** heading, alone and press **Save changes**.

When the page comes back, the new row reads "Saved, ends in" and the last four characters of what you pasted. The default dropdown still says what it said before. "A key went in, and chat didn't move." Press **Change** on the row. Leave the box empty and save, and the key stays. Paste a different key and save, and the ending changes. The **x** at the end of a row removes it and its key.

### 6. Point Embedding, Rerank and Voice at a row (about 2 minutes)

Open **Advanced, Retrieval**. In the embeddings section there's an **Embedding key source** select. Choose the OpenAI row (or whichever matches your embedding provider) and save. Open **RAG Index Status & Reindex**. The first status line shows your embedding provider and "API key set." You didn't paste anything new.

"Same idea on two other Advanced pages." **Reranking and documents** has **Rerank key source**, where a `voyage` row is the usual choice. **Prompt, voice and display** has **Voice and TTS key source**, which offers OpenAI rows only. A heads-up on that last one: choosing a row there is what turns on the speak button, the Conversation Practice chip and the speech-to-text fallback. Adding an OpenAI row for failover alone never does. And a row with a base URL of its own is used only when it's exactly the URL the feature sends to, so check the row's base URL if you expected its key.

### 7. See More options on a course (about 2 minutes)

Open a course's AI settings page. The **Course AI Settings** link is in the course's More menu, and also on **Essentials** for the last course you visited. Provider, API key and model are at the top. Under them is a closed **More options** section.

"Base URL, system prompt, temperature and the monthly spend cap are the settings almost no course changes, so they're tucked away." Open it. Type a temperature of 0.5 and save. The page comes back with **More options** open, because one of the four has a value now. Clear it and save, and it comes back closed.

### 8. Run the usage report (about 3 minutes)

From a shell on the server:

```bash
sudo -u www-data php local/ai_course_assistant/admin/cli/settings_usage_report.php --per-course --tables
```

The first line is a count: how many settings are at their default, how many were changed, how many secrets are set and unset. Under it is one line per changed setting and per secret that is set. Then come the per-course override counts and the row count of each table the plugin owns.

"Look at what it doesn't print. There's no value anywhere, not even for a changed setting. It compares by hash, and for a secret it says only whether one is set." Add `--json` if a script is going to read it. "Run this on Learn or Degrees and you know which settings anyone has ever touched. That's the evidence a later release needs before it removes anything."

### 9. Confirm the page source holds no key (about 1 minute)

Go back to **Models and keys**. View the page source (Ctrl+U, or Cmd+Option+U on a Mac) and search for the first characters of your key, such as `sk-` or `AIza`, and for the ending you saw in "Saved, ends in". The only hit for the ending is that visible text. Search for `type="password"`: every password box has an empty `value`. "Nothing in here to copy, even for someone with the page open." The same holds on **Advanced, Prompt, voice and display** for the Voice providers table.
