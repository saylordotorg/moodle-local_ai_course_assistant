# SOLA v7.12.3: Release Notes and Walkthrough

## Part 1: Release Notes (30 October 2026)

## v7.12.3 (30 October 2026): the anomaly digest stops alerting on small numbers and stops repeating itself

This patch changes only the daily anomaly digest. It adds no setting, table, column or capability, and no upgrade step.

**Negative ratings need a real jump.** The digest used to alert when negative ratings rose by the threshold percentage week over week, so 4 against 1 was "up 300%" on three extra thumbs-down. It now also needs at least 10 negative ratings in the week and at least 5 more than the week before. Token spend and integrity flags are unchanged.

**An alert is not repeated.** The 7-day windows slide a day at a time, so one small bump stayed over the line and the same digest arrived most days. An alert sent in the last 3 days is now skipped unless its value has risen since. The last-sent time and value are kept per alert in the plugin config (`anomaly_digest_last_negative_ratings`, `_token_spend`, `_integrity_flags`).

**The digest says where to look.** The negative-ratings alert lists the courses with the most thumbs-down in the week. The email also carries up to three of the learners' written reasons (120 characters each); the Slack and Teams posts get course names only, because those webhooks leave the site.

**Known limits.** The floors are fixed at 10 ratings and a rise of 5, and the cooldown at 3 days; none is a setting. The existing threshold and dollar-floor settings work as before, and the dollar floor is still off by default.

### Testing

- New `anomaly_digest_noise_test` (8 tests); the digest floor and webhook tests, `cron_tasks_test`, `settings_usage_test`, `secrets_test` and `benchmark_spend_exclusion_test` pass on MySQL. Two existing tests that seeded 6 ratings against 5 now seed 15 against 5.
- CI on the change (#347): all PHPUnit jobs pass; the `pgsql` 8.2 job fails on the plugin-wide code-style check, which was already failing on `main`.
- Jailbreak suite and the manual smoke checklist not run: no chat, prompt or learner-facing path changed.

Plugin version `2026103001`, release `7.12.3`. Change: #347.

## Part 2: Walkthrough (about 2 minutes (admin))

Nothing a learner sees changes.

1. Upgrade from 7.12.2. There is no upgrade step; the plugin version changes only.
2. With the digest enabled, the next time negative ratings rise past the threshold with at least 10 in the week and 5 more than the week before, the email lists the courses with the most thumbs-down and a few written reasons.
3. The same alert does not arrive again for 3 days unless the count goes up.
