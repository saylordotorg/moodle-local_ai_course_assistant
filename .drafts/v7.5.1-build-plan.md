# local_ai_course_assistant v7.5.1 — Soapbox: see your recording, get feedback on how you looked

**Target:** `local_ai_course_assistant` v7.5.1, a patch on top of v7.5.0 (`version.php` currently `2026091704`, release `'7.5.0'`). This ships in the existing plugin, not the `mod_soapbox` extraction.

**Repo root for every path below:** `/Users/tom.caswell/ai-projects/ai_course_assistant`

## 1. What v7.5.1 delivers, in the learner's terms

Today a Saylor learner records a presentation for a Soapbox assignment, sees a row appear in a table that says "Scored", and that is the end of it. The score exists. The feedback exists. Neither is ever shown to them. There is nobody to ask, because these courses are fully online and self-paced.

After v7.5.1 a learner:

1. **Reads how to set up before they record** — camera height, framing, lighting, where to look — instead of guessing, and is told plainly that the video is deleted 7 days after they record it.
2. **Watches their own recording back**, and can **download it** to keep. The existing link opened the video in a browser tab; now there is a real Download that saves a file, and each row shows the exact date that recording disappears.
3. **Reads their actual feedback on the page** — every rubric criterion, its score, and a sentence or two saying what was observed and what to do differently — plus the overall comment and the three next-time tips. All of this was already being generated and written to the database, and none of it was rendered.
4. **Gets feedback on body language and camera presence**, scored from still frames sampled out of their own recording in the browser: gestures that support what they are saying, and habits that distract from it.
5. **Is never scored zero for something that was not looked at.** Audio-only assignment, camera denied, frame capture failed — those two criteria are dropped from the rubric and dropped from the average, and the learner is told in one sentence why that section of feedback is missing.

### Corrections to the project context itself

The brief said several things that the source does not support. Correcting them so the record is right:

1. **"Name the function that computes the total today."** There is none. The total is an inline expression in `classes/external/score_speech.php:282` — `$sum` accumulated in the `foreach ($decoded['criteria'] as $c)` loop, then `(int) round($sum / max(1, count($criteria)))` passed straight into `rubric_manager::save_score()`. Nothing named computes it and nothing else in the plugin computes a speech total.

2. **There is no percentage anywhere in this flow today.** `overall_score` is an integer mean on the 0–5 criterion scale, rendered by `soapbox:overall_badge` = `'Overall {$a}'`. The percentage in section 6 is a *new* behaviour, not a corrected one.

3. **The real risk is worse than "the total is unguarded."** The loop sums whatever the *model* returned, not what the *rubric* asked for. A criterion the model scored 0 because it could not see anything already drags the mean down, with no guard at all. The only reason this has not bitten is that there is currently nothing visual in the rubric to be blind about — which v7.5.1 changes.

4. **Scoring is asynchronous, and the brief's frame-extraction plan is incomplete without that.** `soapbox_finalize_recording::execute()` queues a `score_recording` adhoc task (`soapbox_finalize_recording.php:199-201`); `soapbox_scorer::score_recording()` runs later under cron with no browser anywhere. So browser-extracted frames must be **persisted to object storage before finalize**, exactly as the slide deck already is. "Extract in the browser" is necessary and not sufficient.

5. **`presign_get()` is three lines from supporting downloads.** `soapbox_storage::presign_url()` already accepts an `extraquery` associative array, merges it into the signed query, and `ksort`s before canonicalisation (`soapbox_storage.php`, `presign_url()`). So `response-content-disposition` needs no new signing path — only a parameter on `presign_get()`.

6. **`sbx_assign.rubricid` and `sbx_assign.speaking_level` are dead config.** Both are written by `soapbox_assignment_manager` (lines 114-115 and 158-161) and `speaking_level` is carried by `backup_local_ai_course_assistant_plugin.class.php:175`. Neither is ever read by the scoring path: `score_speech::execute()` resolves the level from `get_config('local_ai_course_assistant', 'soapbox_level_course_' . $courseid)` and ignores the assignment. Two assignments in one course cannot have different levels, whatever the edit form implies. Out of scope for v7.5.1, but recorded here so nobody builds a "per-assignment video rubric" on top of it.

7. **`soapbox:intro`'s "never stored" claim is accurate where it is rendered.** It is rendered only by `soapbox.php` (the standalone practice page), where `score_speech` genuinely stores neither audio nor transcript. `soapbox_present.mustache` never renders it. So `soapbox:intro` is left alone; the presentation flow gets its own, honest copy (`soapbox:present_privacy`).

8. **`soapbox:retention_note` already says the right thing and is shown at the wrong time.** `templates/soapbox_present.mustache:154` renders it inside `{{#hasrecordings}}`, so a learner sees the 7-day promise only *after* they have already recorded. v7.5.1 moves the retention statement above the recorder.

9. **ffmpeg being absent is confirmed as the right constraint, and it is not the binding one.** Even with ffmpeg on the box, the recording lives in S3 and the scorer is a cron task — the browser is still the cheapest place to sample frames, and the sampled sheet still has to be stored.

10. **Locale count:** `ls lang` returns 46 directories (`en` plus 45 others). The brief's "45 other locales" is right.

### Step 10 — `amd/src/soapbox_frames.js` (new)

- `sampleTimes(durationSeconds, count)` — **pure and exported for testing.** Returns `count` timestamps at `d * (0.1 + 0.8 * k / (count - 1))`, i.e. evenly spread across the middle 80%, skipping the first and last tenth where the learner is reaching for the mouse. Returns `[]` for a non-finite or non-positive duration.
- `extractSheet(blob, durationSeconds, opts)` → `Promise<Blob|null>`. Creates a hidden `<video>` from `URL.createObjectURL(blob)`, seeks to each timestamp (set `currentTime`, await `seeked`), draws each frame into a cell of an offscreen `<canvas>`, and resolves `canvas.toBlob(cb, 'image/jpeg', 0.72)`.
  - **6 frames, 3 columns × 2 rows, cell 426×240, sheet 1278×480.** Sizing is a cost decision, see section 7.
  - **`durationSeconds` is passed in, never read from `video.duration`.** For a `MediaRecorder` WebM blob `video.duration` is commonly `Infinity` until a large seek forces a re-index; the recorder already has the true elapsed seconds.

## 5. The default video rubric

`rubric_manager::default_video_criteria()` returns the five existing `DEFAULT_SPEECH_CRITERIA` entries **byte-identical and in order** (Delivery & Fluency, Structure & Organization, Content & Relevance, Language & Vocabulary, Time Management — all `max_score` 5), followed by `VISUAL_CRITERIA`:

```php
/** @var array The two visual criteria, appended to the spoken rubric only when
 *             the attempt actually produced usable video. The 'visual' flag is
 *             what decides whether a criterion is put in the prompt at all; what
 *             is allowed into the SCORE is decided by the allowlist built from
 *             the rubric text in score_speech, which does not depend on it. */
const VISUAL_CRITERIA = [
    [
        'name' => 'Body Language & Gestures',
        'description' => 'Gestures, posture and movement that SUPPORT what you are saying: '
            . 'open hands that mark structure or add emphasis, a steady stance, and weight '
            . 'that stays settled. No repeated habits that DISTRACT from the presentation '
            . '(fidgeting, rocking or pacing, hands in pockets or folded, playing with an '
            . 'object, hair or clothing).',
        'max_score' => 5,
        'visual' => true,
    ],
    [
        'name' => 'Eye Contact & Camera Presence',
        'description' => 'Looking at the camera lens as if it were your audience, rather than '
            . 'reading from notes or watching a second screen. Steady head-and-shoulders '
            . 'framing that keeps your hands in view, a face lit well enough to read, and '
            . 'facial expression that matches what you are saying.',
        'max_score' => 5,
        'visual' => true,

```

Criterion text is **English seed data, not lang strings** — matching every existing `DEFAULT_*` array and the `speech_presets()` docblock, which states that admins edit criterion text on `rubric_admin.php`.

**Two criteria, not one, and not three.** With no instructor anywhere in this flow, one merged criterion forces a single number and one sentence to carry two unrelated remedies: "look at the lens instead of your notes" and "stop clicking the pen" are different actions with different fixes. A learner with strong gestures and no eye contact would get a mid score and generic prose and would have nobody to ask which half to work on. Two is also the ceiling: `compute_overall()` takes an unweighted mean, so every added visual criterion dilutes the five spoken ones, and two is the smallest split that separates the two distinct remedies. Full rubric total 35; spoken-only total 25.

## 6. Scoring maths for the omit-when-unassessed path

`rubric_manager::compute_overall(array $scoredcriteria): array` is pure — no globals, no DB.

Given entries of the shape `['name' => string, 'score' => int, 'feedback' => string, 'assessed' => bool]`, and treating a missing `max_score` as 5 (matching the existing `score_speech` default):

- An entry is **excluded** when `isset($c['assessed']) && $c['assessed'] === false`.
- An entry with **no `assessed` key counts as assessed** — backward compatibility with every score row written before v7.5.1, and with any provider that ignores the field.
- `assessed` = the count of included entries.
- `overall` = `(int) round($sum / $assessed)`, and **`0` when `$assessed === 0`** — an explicit branch, not `max(1, ...)`, so no division by zero and no fake score.
- `maxtotal` = the sum of `max_score` over the **included** entries only.
- `pct` = `(int) round(100 * $sum / $maxtotal)`, and **`0` when `$maxtotal === 0`**.

`overall` remains an integer mean on the 0–5 criterion scale, so `overall_score` (`int(3)`) and the existing `soapbox:overall_badge` = `'Overall {$a}'` are unchanged for every historic row. `pct` is new, lives only in `session_meta`, and needs no migration.

### Worked example: 7 criteria, 2 unassessed

A learner records with the camera pointing at the ceiling. The frame sheet uploads, the vision pass runs, and the model reports it cannot judge either visual criterion.

| Criterion | max | score | assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery & Fluency | 5 | 4 | yes |
| Structure & Organization | 5 | 3 | yes |
| Content & Relevance | 5 | 5 | yes |
| Language & Vocabulary | 5 | 4 | yes |
| Time Management | 5 | 3 | yes |
| Body Language & Gestures | 5 | 0 | **no** |
| Eye Contact & Camera Presence | 5 | 0 | **no** |

- `sum` = 4 + 3 + 5 + 4 + 3 = **19** (the two zeros are never added)
- `assessed` = **5**
- `maxtotal` = 5 × 5 = **25**
- `overall` = round(19 / 5) = round(3.8) = **4**
- `pct` = round(100 × 19 / 25) = **76**

The learner sees: five criteria with scores and feedback; two rows greyed with a "Not assessed" badge; *"Your overall score is the average of the 5 criteria that were assessed, out of 7 in the rubric (76%). Anything that could not be assessed was left out rather than scored zero."*; and the `soapbox:visual_not_assessed` sentence appended to the overall comment.

**What v7.5.0's code would have produced from the same response:** `round(19 / 7)` = **3**, and 19/35 = 54%. A whole point of overall score, and 22 percentage points, taken off a learner for a camera angle, with nobody to appeal to. That gap is the regression `tests/score_total_exclusion_test.php` exists to pin.

## 7. Added cost per scored recording

One extra vision call per **scored video** recording. Audio-mode assignments, camera-denied recordings, and any recording where frame extraction failed cost nothing extra — `frames_key` is null and `soapbox_gesture_vision::observe()` returns before it touches a provider.

Sizing, at the default `soapbox_vision_model` of `gpt-4o-mini`:

- One JPEG contact sheet, **1278 × 480** (6 frames, 3 × 2, cells 426 × 240, quality 0.72). Typically 90–160 KB.
- OpenAI tiles that image at 512 px: `ceil(1278/512) = 3` by `ceil(480/512) = 1` → **3 tiles**.
- `gpt-4o-mini` image tokens = 2,833 base + 5,667 per tile = 2,833 + 17,001 = **19,834 input tokens**, plus roughly 200 tokens of system prompt.
- Output capped at `max_tokens => 300`.

At list pricing ($0.15 / 1M input, $0.60 / 1M output):

- input ≈ 20,034 × $0.15/1M = **$0.0030**
- output ≈ 300 × $0.60/1M = **$0.00018**
- **≈ $0.0032 per scored video recording**, about a third of a US cent.

The scoring prompt itself grows by up to ~2,000 characters of visual evidence (≈500 tokens) plus the anti-guess and no-teacher sentences — under $0.0002 at the same rate, and nil on audio-only attempts.

At Saylor's default cap of 3 recordings per assignment, the ceiling is **under one cent per learner per assignment**. Every row is attributed through `log_ancillary_usage($provider, $userid, $courseid, 'gesture_vision', ...)` with the learner's id, so it appears in the existing spend attribution under its own label and can be separated from `slide_vision` and `speech_score`. The grid geometry is the cost dial: a 4 × 2 sheet at the same cell size crosses into 4 tiles and costs about 30% more.

Storage: one ~130 KB object per recording for at most 7 days, alongside a video that is measured in tens of megabytes. Immaterial.

## 9. What this does not do

- **Nobody reviews any of this.** There is no teacher, no moderation and no appeal anywhere in the flow. Every word the learner reads is generated by a model and shipped unread. If the feedback is wrong about a learner's gestures, nothing in this design catches it — the mitigations are that the visual criteria are dropped rather than guessed when evidence is thin, and that the two criteria are separated so a wrong call on one does not contaminate the other.
- **Six stills are not video.** A habit that happens between frames is invisible. Pacing, rocking and a fidget that starts at 4:10 and stops at 4:25 can all be missed entirely. The scoring is honest about sampling in the prompt, but a learner cannot tell from the feedback whether a habit was absent or merely unsampled.
- **No motion, no audio-visual sync, no eye tracking.** "Eye contact" means "the eyes are directed at the lens in these stills". A learner who glances down between every sampled moment scores well on it.
- **It depends on the learner framing themselves.** This is the largest learner-dependency in the release, and the reason the setup instructions exist. A camera that cuts off the hands makes the body-language criterion unassessable, and the only person who can fix that is the learner, before the next take. The `soapbox:visual_not_assessed` sentence is the whole remediation path.
- **Download depends on the learner acting inside 7 days.** `soapbox_cleanup` deletes the object on `expires_at` and there is no recovery. The page states the exact date per recording and tells them to download what they want to keep; nothing else protects them.
- **The frames are stills of the learner, stored in the same bucket for the same 7 days.** They are covered by retention cleanup, by GDPR erasure and by the privacy metadata, but they are one more copy of the learner's face that did not exist before v7.5.1. That is a real, if small, increase in exposure and it is the price of the feature.
- **Browsers that cannot seek a `MediaRecorder` blob, or lack `canvas.toBlob`, silently produce no visual feedback.** The learner gets the `soapbox:visual_not_assessed` sentence, which names a camera problem they do not have. Distinguishing "browser could not sample" from "camera was off" would take another finalize field and is deliberately not in this release.
- **`sbx_assign.rubricid` and `sbx_assign.speaking_level` are still dead.** The level still comes from `soapbox_level_course_<courseid>`, so two assignments in one course still cannot have different levels or different rubrics, whatever the edit form implies. v7.5.1 does not widen that, and deliberately does not build on it.
- **`soapbox:intro` is unchanged.** Its "audio and the transcript are never stored" claim is accurate on `soapbox.php`, the only page that renders it. The presentation flow gets its own honest copy rather than a rewrite that would make the standalone page's text vaguer than the truth.
- **The other 45 locales ship untranslated for these keys.** Moodle falls back to English per key, so nothing breaks, but a Spanish-speaking learner reads the setup instructions and the "not assessed" explanation in English until the translation pass lands.
