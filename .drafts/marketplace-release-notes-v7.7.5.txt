Version 7.7.5 (build 2026100116)

Opening the assistant no longer covers Moodle's own side drawers. On Moodle 5.3,
which floats the course index beside the content on course pages, opening the
assistant moved the content under the course index. On 4.5 and 5.3, the
right-hand block drawer slid under the assistant. The assistant now lays out
around Moodle's drawers whenever it, or a drawer, opens or closes, and puts
everything back when it closes.

Checked on Moodle 5.3 across course, activity and participant pages with and
without the block drawer, and on Moodle 4.5. Continuous integration covers PHP
8.1 through 8.3 against Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
