Q: What is Saylor University? | A: A nonprofit working since 2008 to offer free and open online courses to anyone who wants to learn. Over 100 full-length college and professional courses; more than two million students served worldwide. https://www.saylor.org/about
Q: How do I contact Saylor University support? | A: Use the "Submit a request" link at the top of any help center page, or email contact@saylor.org. We aim to reply within 24 hours. https://support.saylor.org/hc/en-us/requests/new
Q: How much does Saylor University cost? | A: Nothing. There is no tuition and there are no fees; courses and course completion certificates are free. Saylor Direct Credit exams are covered on the program pages. https://www.saylor.org/credit
Q: What courses do you offer? | A: Over 100 courses. Popular topics include ESL (English as a Second Language), Bitcoin, Business Administration, and Computer Science. Full catalog: https://learn.saylor.org/course/index.php
Q: How do I get started? | A: Create a free account and enroll yourself in any course from the catalog. Courses are self-paced, so you can start whenever you like. https://learn.saylor.org/course/index.php
Q: How do I contact my instructor? | A: Saylor University courses have no live instructors or teaching assistants. Professors and subject matter experts build each course for self-paced study.
Q: When can I take my exam? | A: Whenever you are ready. The Certificate Final Exam sits at the end of the course, after the final unit.
Q: Are your certificates free? | A: Yes. Course completion certificates cost nothing.
Q: How do I earn my course completion certificate? | A: Pass the course's Certificate Final Exam, usually with a score of 70% or higher.
Q: Are your certificates recognized? | A: They carry no specific endorsement, but they work well on a resume, CV, or LinkedIn profile. Students in nearly every country have earned them. https://www.saylor.org/certificates
Q: How do I verify my enrollment? | A: Saylor University is not an accredited school and has no part-time or full-time enrollment status, so enrollment verification is usually not available.
Q: How do I take a course for college credit? | A: Some courses carry a credit recommendation from the American Council on Education (ACE). Pass the proctored Saylor Direct Credit Final Exam for an eligible course to earn the recommendation; your college decides whether to accept it. https://www.saylor.org/credit
Q: I have a question that wasn't answered here. | A: Use the "Submit a request" link at the top of the help center page and send us a message. https://support.saylor.org/hc/en-us/requests/new
