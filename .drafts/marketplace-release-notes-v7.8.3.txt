Version 7.8.3 (build 2026100803)

Practice quiz wrong answers are cleaner. On 15 course pages, 53% of Claude Haiku 4.5's questions had an absolute word ("all", "only", "exact", "eliminate") in a wrong choice, which tells a learner it is wrong without knowing the subject. Those choices are now rewritten, and the same pass fixes the answer-key length: the correct answer was the longest choice on 72% of Haiku questions before 7.8.2, 64% after it, and is now 29% (about 25% is chance). With Claude Sonnet 5.5 the absolute-word rate went from 20% to 0% and the longest-key rate from 52% to 37%. A second request handles the hard cases, and the better of two attempts is kept. No wrong keys were added, and the number a reviewer judged ambiguous stayed within noise.

Continuous integration covers PHP 8.1 through 8.3 against Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
