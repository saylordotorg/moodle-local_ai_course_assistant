Version 7.8.3 (build 2026100803)

Quiz answer-key balancing is stronger. In a measured run on 15 course pages, the correct answer was still the longest choice on 64% of Claude Haiku 4.5 questions after 7.8.2 (72% before); it is now 36% (about 25% is chance). With Claude Sonnet 5.5 it went from 50% to 32%. No questions had a wrong key, and the number a reviewer judged ambiguous did not change. Distractors that are far shorter than the correct answer are now rewritten with an explicit length target, with a second request for the ones that are still too short, and the better of two attempts is kept.

Continuous integration covers PHP 8.1 through 8.3 against Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
