Version 7.7.2 (build 2026100113)

On a phone, the assistant's text box could refuse to take typing. With Moodle's
menu drawer open behind the assistant, every tap in the text box sent the
cursor back to that drawer, because Moodle locks focus inside an open drawer on
small screens. The assistant now closes Moodle's open drawers when it opens and
whenever it's tapped.

The assistant no longer opens by itself outside a course. On the dashboard,
profiles and the site home it used to follow the auto-open setting meant for
courses. Auto-open now applies to course pages only.

Tested: 2,078 automated tests with no failures, the security validator corpus at
36 of 36, and PHP lint clean across 611 files. Continuous integration covers PHP
8.1 through 8.3 against Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
