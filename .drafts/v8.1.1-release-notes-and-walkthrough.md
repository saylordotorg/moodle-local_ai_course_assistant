## Part 1: Release Notes

## v8.1.1: Voice models priced from the vendors' own price lists

**Why.** 8.1.0 added Gemini text to speech but no Gemini speech price, and left a known bug: `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` matched the `gemini-2.5-flash` chat price and was billed as chat text. The same trap caught `gpt-4o-mini-tts` (priced as `gpt-4o-mini` chat), `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` and `gpt-4o-transcribe` (priced as chat, about 20 times too low), and `gpt-4o-audio-preview`.

**Audio models never take a text model's price.** A model whose name has an audio part (`tts`, `audio`, `realtime`, `live`, `transcribe`, `whisper`, `stt`, `speech`) can now only be priced by an audio price row, or by the `selfhosted-` row (free). An audio model with no audio row shows as unpriced in the model registry banner and the daily price-drift check instead of being quietly billed as text. The rule applies to every layer, including registry rows an admin types, and the drift check uses the same rule when it decides whether a vendor's model is "already priced".

**New prices** (USD per 1M tokens, built into the plugin):

| gemini-3.8-live, -live-extended-thinking, gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview | 3.00 audio | 12.00 audio | Google pricing page, 2026-11-21 |

tts-1 ($15 per 1M characters) and tts-1-hd ($30) and whisper-1 ($0.006/min) were confirmed unchanged. Realtime and Live rows carry the audio rates, the dearer pair; text rates are in the code comments.

**The January 2027 step is automatic.** Google lists higher 2027 prices for both Gemini TTS models without a day; the other Gemini 3.x rows step up on January 1, so the plugin switches at 2027-01-01 00:00 UTC. Pricing uses today's date, not the date of each usage row, because the spend queries price token totals per model. So from January 1, December's Gemini TTS usage is re-priced at the new rate wherever it is summed again (for example a 30-day dashboard in early January). That over-counts history and never under-counts, which is the safe side for spend caps; monthly caps start on the 1st and are exact. A registry row an admin types for one of these models is NOT stepped.

**Usage that doesn't separate text from audio is estimated high, never as text only.**
- Text to speech: OpenAI's speech endpoint returns no token counts, so a gpt-4o-mini-tts row holds only the text estimate. The cost now adds 10 audio tokens per text token at the audio rate. The 10 is derived from Gemini's 25 audio tokens a second and a speaking rate of 12 to 15 characters a second (6.7 to 8.3), rounded up; OpenAI's audio tokens per second for gpt-4o-mini-tts were not collected, so the same figure is an unverified assumption there. The same applies to a Gemini row when Google omits the audio count. tts-1 and tts-1-hd are priced per character as before.
- Realtime: the realtime logger writes every session as `gpt-4o-realtime` with duration x 50 tokens and no split; those tokens are now priced at the $20 output rate instead of the $5 input rate, so realtime spend reads four times higher than before.

**Price feeds can't undo audio prices.** LiteLLM's per-token fields are text prices, so the weekly LiteLLM refresh and the LiteLLM price source now skip audio models, and the registry ignores any LiteLLM ("upstream") row for an audio model a site already has. A row an admin types, or applies from the drift check, still wins.

**Not priced on purpose** (they show in the unpriced banner if used): gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts, gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts, gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview and gemini-2.5-flash-native-audio-* (retiring, no official price collected); gpt-live-1 and gpt-live-1.1 (billed per active and standby minute, which no SOLA usage row records); grok-tts and grok-stt (no price collected).

**Known gap.** sse.php writes realtime usage under `gpt-4o-realtime` whatever model the session used, so the new gpt-realtime prices only apply once the logger records the real model. Priced at the gpt-4o-realtime $20 rate, a gpt-realtime-2.1 session may still read lower than its bill (not measured).

**Upgrade notes.** No schema change, no upgrade step, no new strings or settings. Spend figures change at once for any site with realtime, OpenAI TTS or transcription traffic (higher), and for gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts or gpt-4o-audio-preview traffic (now unpriced instead of priced as chat).

### Testing

- New: `tests/voice_pricing_test.php` (19 tests): every new rate exactly, the prefix-collision regression, text-family registry rows unable to price audio, LiteLLM audio rows skipped and upstream audio rows ignored, the drift comparison, the 2027 step at 23:59:59 and 00:00:00 UTC including the in-request cache, admin rows not stepped, the unseparated-usage estimates, unknown voice models unpriced (never free), and the unpriced banner. Two were proven by planting the defect in a private copy: removing the audio filter fails the regression and banner tests; removing the schedule check from the cache fails the 2027 test.
- Existing pricing and spend tests re-run, all passing (see the commit for the list); full plugin suite results are in the commit message.
- Not run: CI (Postgres), any live vendor call, a dev-site deploy.

## Part 2: Walkthrough (about 3 minutes)

Open **Site administration, Plugins, Local plugins, AI Course Assistant, Model registry**.

"Search for gpt-realtime-mini. The price is the audio price from OpenAI's page, $10 in and $20 out per million tokens, with the source and date in the code. Search for gemini-3.8-flash-tts: 50 cents text in, $9 audio out. On January 1 it becomes $1 and $18 by itself."

"Now look at the unpriced-models banner. If this site has played any gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts audio, that model is listed here now. In 8.1.0 it was hidden, because it borrowed the chat price of gemini-2.5-flash. A speech model never borrows a chat price any more; it is either priced as speech or listed as unpriced."

"If you want to price one of the retiring models until it shuts down, add a row here with Google's audio price. Rows you add win over the built-in prices and are not changed by the January step."

Open **Token analytics** and filter to voice. "Realtime and OpenAI speech costs are higher than last week. That's the estimate being made on the safe side: when a usage record doesn't say how much audio came back, the plugin assumes more, not less."
