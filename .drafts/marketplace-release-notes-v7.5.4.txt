Version 7.5.4 (build 2026092500)

A single-defect release. The help panel was printing internal engineering notes to learners, and had been since v7.4.7.

FIXED: THE HELP PANEL SHOWED INTERNAL COMMENTS

A Mustache comment ends at the first closing brace pair it meets, not at a matching one. The comment above the help panel quoted a template tag inline, while explaining why that tag could not be used there, so the comment ended at that point. The remaining nine lines rendered to the page as body text: a paragraph about language switching, on-demand string fetching and bundle size, ending in a stray brace pair, immediately above the real first line of the help text.

Anyone who pressed the question-mark button in the assistant header saw it.

Nothing in the build could have caught this. The Mustache linter checks syntax, and the template is syntactically valid; the comment simply ends earlier than its author intended, and everything after it is ordinary template text as far as a parser is concerned. A new test now scans every template and fails the build on any comment truncated by a tag written inside it. The fix is to describe tags in words rather than writing them, and the comment now opens by saying so.

The defect was introduced in 7.4.7 and was present in every release through 7.5.3.
