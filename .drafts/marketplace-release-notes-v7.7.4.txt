Version 7.7.4 (build 2026100115)

Answers reloaded from chat history no longer show a stray space before full
stops and commas. SOLA marks claims taken from course content with an inline
citation, and the copy saved for history removed those markers but left the
space in front of each one. New answers are saved cleanly; answers saved
before this release keep their spaces.

Tested on Moodle 5.3 as a learner, with new automated cases for the citation
shapes seen there. Continuous integration covers PHP 8.1 through 8.3 against
Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
