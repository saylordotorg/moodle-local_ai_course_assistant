## v7.7.2 (5 October 2026): learners can type into the assistant on a phone again, and it stays closed outside courses

**On a phone, tapping the assistant's text box could send the cursor somewhere else.**
If Moodle's own menu drawer was open behind the assistant, every tap in the
text box moved focus back to that drawer's close button, so nothing could be
typed. Moodle locks focus inside an open drawer on small screens, and the
assistant sits outside it. The assistant now closes Moodle's open drawers when
it opens and whenever it's tapped, and it does so without Moodle moving focus
back to its menu button afterwards.

**The assistant no longer opens by itself outside a course.** On the
dashboard, profiles and the site home it runs against the support course, so it
picked up that course's auto-open setting, or the global one, and opened over
pages the learner came to for something else. Auto-open now applies to course
pages only.
