Version 7.7.6 (build 2026100117)

Answers from Gemini no longer stop mid-sentence. Gemini 2.5 Flash thinks before
it answers, and that thinking used to come out of the same Max tokens allowance
as the answer, so long thinking left the answer cut short. Thinking now has its
own allowance. An answer that does run out of room is stored as truncated and
tells the learner it was cut short, for Claude as well as Gemini.

Gemini's thinking is now counted in spend. Google bills it as output, and the
token analytics, spend caps, anomaly check and spend export now include it, so
reported Gemini spend will rise to match the invoice. Check per-course spend
caps after upgrading.

Tables, block quotes, horizontal rules, strikethrough and smaller headings now
render in the chat. Everything in them is escaped like ordinary text.

Continuous integration covers PHP 8.1 through 8.3 against Moodle 4.5, 5.0 and
5.1, on both MariaDB and PostgreSQL.
